# Demo video script (≈3 minutes)

Record on a phone-sized browser window (or an iPhone with the PWA installed) at https://plain.lengway.me. Sign in before recording so the wallet already exists, and keep a second tab with Solana Explorer (devnet) open.

## 0:00–0:20 · Problem
**Screen:** landing page, scroll slowly.
**Voice:** "Tokenized Treasuries are a multi-billion-dollar market on Solana, but only crypto-native people can reach them. Most savers in emerging markets still keep money in local-currency deposits or cash dollars. RWA Bridge is a seamless yield layer: email in, dollars in, yield out — and every step is on-chain."

## 0:20–0:45 · Sign in and add money
**Screen:** **Начать → Войти по email** (already signed in: show the greeting), then **Пополнить → Тестовый банкомат → Получить $100**. Show the success screen and tap **Открыть в обозревателе**.
**Voice:** "Sign in with email, a Solana smart wallet is created for you. Here I add $100 of test dollars — the explorer shows the mint to my wallet."

## 0:45–1:15 · Card on-ramp
**Screen:** **Пополнить → Карта · Transak**, show the quote under the button ("≈ 47 USDC · комиссия $3"), open the sheet, show the prefilled test flow, close.
**Voice:** "In production the same button is a real card purchase of USDC. Transak and MoonPay open inside the app; we verify the order on our server before crediting."

## 1:15–1:50 · Earn
**Screen:** **Доход → Долларовый вклад**: live APY with source and date, T-bill comparison. **Вложить $100**, sign, success: "Вы получили 99,99 vUSDY-DEMO". Open **Портфель**: allocation bar, the vUSDY-DEMO row, token account and explorer link.
**Voice:** "The deposit is a vault share token in my wallet. Its price follows the live USDY yield, so the value grows on-chain, not in a database."

## 1:50–2:15 · Real asset share
**Screen:** **Активы → Офисное здание (демо-объект) → Купить $10**, then **Операции в сети** in the portfolio.
**Voice:** "Fractional real estate works the same way: pay from the balance, receive asset tokens. Everything is visible in the on-chain history."

## 2:15–2:40 · Inside a bank
**Screen:** `/demo-bank`: services grid, tap **Инвестиции**, **Пополнить → Со счёта в Демо-банке**, bank confirmation in tenge, **Оплатить**, balance updates.
**Voice:** "For banks we are a layer, not a competitor. The bank keeps KYC and tenge; the user confirms the payment in the bank's own UI and gets on-chain yield."

## 2:40–3:00 · Withdraw and close
**Screen:** **Вывести → Из вклада на баланс**, success; then show the receipt email on the phone.
**Voice:** "Withdraw any time: shares go back, dollars come out, and you get a receipt with the transaction link. RWA Bridge — real-world yield, as simple as a bank deposit."

## Shot checklist
- Landing hero and the "Всё в сети" block with mint addresses
- Test ATM success + explorer
- Transak quote and sheet
- Vault deposit success with share amount
- Portfolio allocation bar and holding detail
- Office share purchase
- Demo bank confirmation sheet
- Withdrawal and the receipt email
